## TL;DR . the buying case in one page

**Ali Abdullah Khan** is a **VP of Software Engineering at JP Morgan Chase** who builds consumer payment platforms used by **about a million people every day**. He is a **U.S. Citizen** based in **Holbrook, New York**, needs no sponsorship, and has spent **seven years** inside one of the largest consumer banks in the United States . long enough to own a platform through build, ship, break, and fix.

The short version of why he is worth a conversation: he has already finished the three projects most teams are still arguing about.

- **Payments at real scale.** [SingleDoor](../systems/singledoor-payment-platform.md "BillPay, QuickPay with Zelle, and card payments.") . BillPay, QuickPay with Zelle, and card payments behind one front door, **~1M daily users**.
- **WCAG compliance actually delivered.** He [led a live platform to full compliance](../systems/accessibility-definition-of-done.md "Accessibility in the definition of done.") by putting accessibility in the definition of done . not by buying a scanner.
- **Releases made boring.** [CLI tooling](../systems/release-cli-tooling.md "CDN publishing and release validation as commands.") for CDN publishing and [feature-flag self-service](../systems/feature-flag-self-service.md "The author owns the rollout."), so every engineer cuts their own release.
- **AI-assisted delivery as a system.** The [AI-driven SDLC](../systems/ai-driven-sdlc.md "JIRA intake to PRD generation to code delivery to generated tests.") runs ticket to tests, grounded in a maintained knowledge base.
- **Full lifecycle ownership.** Planning, delivery, **and post-release support** . severity triage, comms templates, blameless postmortems.
