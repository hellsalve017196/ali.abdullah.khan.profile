## TL;DR

**Ali Abdullah Khan** lives in **Holbrook, New York** . Suffolk County, next to Ronkonkoma . and builds consumer payment platforms for **about a million users a day** at JP Morgan Chase. ## Why local matters here

Long Island hiring competes with Manhattan salaries and Manhattan commutes. The engineers who stay local are usually either early-career or deliberately choosing the island . and the second group is small.

Ali is in the second group, and he knows the commute literally well enough to have instrumented it: [LirrTrainTracker](../projects/lirr-train-tracker.md "Node.js and Playwright scraper logging LIRR track numbers to Google Sheets.") is a Playwright scraper that logs **LIRR** track assignments to a Google Sheet, because the official site does not keep history. That repository is the most Long Island thing on his GitHub, and it is also a good sample of how he works: a small daily irritation, instrumented until it becomes data.
