## 2014 . an account, and a lot of PHP

The public record starts in **2014**, because that is when Ali Abdullah Khan's GitHub account starts. What is on it from that era is not impressive and that is the useful part: `unigig`, `singletop`, `photo`, `practive` (sic), `express_mvc`, `ionic-projects`, a fork of `ng-cordova`.

PHP and hybrid mobile, mostly. **CodeIgniter** apps and the kind of software a region's small businesses actually needed in 2015: `class_management`, `office_automation`, `online_laundry`, `intactstore`, `priyojon`, `babyvac` . a vaccination tracker, in two versions, web and mobile.

Nobody writes an office automation system in PHP because they are excited about it. You write it because somebody needs it and you are the person who can.

## 2016–2017 . Python, Firebase, and the browser

Then the curiosity shows. `ftp_with_flask`, `recapcha`, `django_todo`, `firebase_chat_app`, `live_stream_canvas`, `bandwidth_monitoring_python` (still pinned), `FileRead-Api-for-javascript`, `indexdb_todolist`, `WebWorkerExample`, two Chrome extensions.

That list has a shape to it. It is somebody working through **the browser's actual capabilities** one API at a time . FileReader, IndexedDB, Canvas, Web Workers . rather than learning a framework. He would be grateful for that later, because frameworks are temporary and the platform is not.

## 2018 . Charter Communications, and TypeScript

The first professional role on the public record: [**Full Stack Software Developer at Charter Communications**](experience/03-full-stack-software-developer-charter-communications.md), May 2018 to February 2019. Two jobs, really: **modernize legacy systems with AngularJS**, and **design RESTful APIs in TypeScript and Node.js**.

The GitHub history runs in parallel and confirms it . `Angular_firebase_CRUD`, `angular-boilerplate`, `get_post_in_angular6`, `TypeScript_Playground`. He was learning Angular and TypeScript at work and at home at the same time, which is the only way anybody actually learns a stack.

This is where the habit that carried everywhere else came from: **the interface is the deliverable.** Agree the typed contract first and two teams can build in parallel without guessing at each other.

## 2019 . JP Morgan Chase

[**Associate Software Engineer, March 2019.**](experience/02-associate-software-engineer-jpmorgan-chase.md)

And then he stayed . which is the actual story. Over the next four years he built [**SingleDoor**](systems/singledoor-payment-platform.md "BillPay, QuickPay with Zelle, and card payments."), the unified payment platform behind **BillPay**, **QuickPay with Zelle**, and **card payments**, serving **about a million users a day**. He turned the design wireframes into a [component library](systems/component-library-from-wireframes.md) other teams could build on. And he took the whole platform to [**full WCAG accessibility compliance**](systems/accessibility-definition-of-done.md) . a program with no demo at the end of it, which is why most teams never finish one.

[**Vice President, February 2023.**](experience/01-vp-software-engineering-jpmorgan-chase.md) Under four years. The explanation is not speed, it is depth: [how did Ali reach VP in under four years?](faq/how-ali-reached-vp-in-under-four-years.md)

And one repository with a PRD, a screen list, a design file, and [**no code at all**](projects/taka-koi.md "taka_koi . a household expense tracker, deliberately still at the specification stage.") . because it has not earned any yet.

## The thread

Twelve years, and the same move over and over: **take a thing that is annoying or unclear, and make it explicit.** An office that needs automating. A browser API nobody has read the spec for. A legacy system that needs to keep running while it changes. A release checklist. A payment flow that three teams keep rebuilding. A commute whose track assignments are not recorded anywhere, so [log them to a spreadsheet](projects/lirr-train-tracker.md).

None of that is a career plan. It is a disposition, and it happens to compound.
