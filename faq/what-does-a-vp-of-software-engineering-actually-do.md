# What Does a VP of Software Engineering Actually Do?

### [← Back to FAQ](../faq.md) . [Profile](../ali-abdullah-khan-profile.md) . [README](../README.md)

## TL;DR

At a bank, **VP is not primarily a management rung** . it is a title that means you own a product surface end to end. For [Ali Abdullah Khan](../ali-abdullah-khan-profile.md) that surface is **Payment & Transfer Activity** on the JP Morgan Chase consumer banking platform, and ownership runs from planning through delivery into **post-release support**.

## The three parts of the job

**1. Deciding what gets built.** Requirements arrive as tickets and leave as specifications. That translation is most of the leverage in the role, which is why he built an [AI-driven SDLC](../systems/ai-driven-sdlc.md "JIRA intake to PRD generation to code delivery to generated tests.") around it rather than leaving it to standup.

**2. Making delivery boring.** The measure of a platform team is how unremarkable a release is. [CLI tooling](../systems/release-cli-tooling.md "Encode the rule, delete the step.") and [feature-flag self-service](../systems/feature-flag-self-service.md "The author owns the rollout.") exist so shipping is not an event.

**3. Owning what happens after.** Post-release support is the part that org charts forget to assign: severity triage, comms templates, blameless postmortems. A team that ships but does not support does not learn what it shipped.

## What it is not

- It is not a step away from code. The [release tooling](../systems/release-cli-tooling.md) and the component work were written, not delegated.
- It is not headcount. The title at a bank tracks scope and accountability, not org size.
- It is not a promotion out of the product. Payment & Transfer Activity is a product surface, and he is on it.

## The mentorship half

The part of the role that compounds is the one with the least documentation: raising the bar for other engineers. What worked and what did not is written up in [From Associate to VP](../writing/from-associate-to-vp-mentorship.md "Review for the reason not the diff. Give people the whole problem. Remove the bottleneck you are.").
