## The five recurring themes

1. **AI-assisted development** . the knowledge base is the product; the model is the interface.
2. **Accessibility-first engineering** . a definition-of-done problem, not a CSS problem.
3. **Release automation** . feature flags and CLI tooling, until deploys are boring.
4. **Scale discipline** . at a million users a day the fundamentals hold and the failure modes change.
5. **Mentorship and leadership** . including the practices that did not work.
