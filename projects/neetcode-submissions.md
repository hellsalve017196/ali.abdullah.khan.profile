A VP of Software Engineering with seven years at a large bank does not need to grind algorithm problems. Committing them publicly is the point: **it is a learning log that cannot be quietly abandoned.**

It also fills a real gap. Platform work at scale is mostly about failure modes, release safety, and component design . none of which keeps graph algorithms sharp. The DSA track is deliberate maintenance on a skill the day job does not exercise.
